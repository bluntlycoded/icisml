Call for Papers

ICISML 2026 invites submissions of original, high-quality research papers in the following tracks. We look forward to your contributions!

Track 1: Image Recognition

Cooperative intelligent applications, pattern recognition, embedded systems, UAVs, adaptive & autonomous robotics, real-time intelligence, and more.

Track 2: Machine Learning

Neural networks, deep learning, hybrid and non-linear systems, large-scale data analytics, learning algorithms, and emerging ML frameworks.

Track 3: Intelligent Systems & ML Applications

Smart cities, Internet of Things, healthcare informatics, speech recognition, natural language processing, data mining, and advanced ML applications.

Track 4: Intelligent Communication Networks

Cognitive radio, vehicular networks, sensor-based intelligence, 5G & 6G technologies, UAV communication, underwater networks, and cloud-edge integration.

Track 5: Bioinformatics & Biotechnology

Genomics, proteomics, computational biology, biomedical signal analysis, drug discovery, genetic engineering, and AI in biotechnology.

Track 6: Agricultural Engineering

Smart farming, precision agriculture, agri-robotics, IoT in agriculture, remote sensing for crop monitoring, and sustainable agricultural systems.

Submission Guidelines

  • Papers must be original, not under review elsewhere.
  • Follow the ICISML 2026 template for formatting.
  • Submissions must be in English, with a maximum of 8 pages.
  • All papers will undergo a rigorous peer-review process.
  • Accepted papers will be published in the conference proceedings.
