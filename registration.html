Conference Registration Fee

Secure your spot at ICISML 2025 by registering according to your preferred category. The table below outlines the registration fees for conference proceedings and journal publication.

Registration Types Registration Fees
Conference Proceedings 6,000 INR
Journal Publication 20,000 INR (Indian Author)
550 USD (Others)

How to Register

  1. Fill out the online registration form.
  2. Make the payment via our secure payment gateway or bank transfer.
  3. Receive a confirmation email with further instructions.
