About ICISML 2025 & NIT Meghalaya

ICISML 2025 is the International Conference on Intelligent Communication Systems and Machine Learning, bringing together scholars, researchers, and practitioners to share cutting-edge research and insights in these dynamic fields.

About the Conference

The conference aims to foster collaboration, innovation, and knowledge sharing among experts and enthusiasts. With a blend of keynote sessions, technical presentations, and interactive workshops, ICISML 2025 promises a rich platform for academic and professional growth.

About NIT Meghalaya

National Institute of Technology Meghalaya is one of the premier institutes in India, dedicated to excellence in technical education and research. Established under the NIT Act, the institute is committed to nurturing talent and driving innovation in science and engineering.
